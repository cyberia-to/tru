Companion of `specs/rewards.md` as completed on 2026-11-04 (the owner: "the proof of work must really sample values by the rewards distribution; a completely finished rewards specification, without notes about what is undone; change every document that contradicts it"). Every clause below was read at its origin head on 2026-11-04 (`git -C ~/cyber/<repo> fetch; git show origin/<default>:<path>`); line numbers are of that revision. Old text is verbatim.
