## 1. what is settled

One epoch from the genesis graph `A_prev = G` to `A_cur = net_axons(G, positions)`, settled by the exact rule of rewards.md §7 and chain-economy §5: the four-node path gradient (`tru::focusing::epoch::path_gradient`, `EpochParams::default()`), `a(c) = Δs·σ·ḡ`, `x(c) = max(0, a(c))` (every position is a new high on an unstaked side, so `N/R = 1`), and `pay(c) = min(⌊Δs/100⌋, ⌊B · x / X⌋)` with the budget `B = ⌊F/4⌋` of the focus pool (chain-economy §5.5, `POOL_RELEASE = 4`). The pool is fed per block by `cut = SUBSIDY · min(5,000, isqrt(active_{E−1} · 10^8 / supply)) / 10^4`, reading the previous epoch's active stake (§6.1) — here the honest swarm's, the cartel's stake arriving in the epoch settled — at the supply of the settlement height `2L + D`; two pool states: the steady state (four epochs of accrual, one paid: `B` = one epoch's inflow) and after ten quiet epochs (`B` = 3.5 inflows).

The honest swarm: `honest` positions (64, then 256) raising existing axons by 30 % with valence +1 (the receipt's players of tru#46), active in the previous epoch. The cartel: a ring of `k` colluders, `k ∈ {2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64}`, each locking `s ∈ {10^11, 10^12, 10^13, 10^14}` with valence +1 on one axon of a cycle over `k` fresh particles; `anchored` adds one link of stake `s` from the heaviest existing particle (by out-weight) into the ring. Beside the exact payout, the external-attention gate that rewards.md §7 carried on 2026-11-12 (`x` scaled by `min(1, ext/int)`, `ext` the pre-existing weight entering the position's region — the closure of its endpoints under the epoch's new axons — from outside, `int` the stake this epoch's positions put on axons into it), under the steady budget: the measurement that decided against it. Integers throughout; floats only in the printing.

## 2. reading

- A ring of fresh particles with no link from the existing graph earns nothing at every `k` and every `s` of the sweep, under the exact rule alone: its attribution is ≤ 0 — new particles spread the focus and `J = D_KL(φ ‖ u)` falls. This is a property of the measure within the sweep (`R ≤ 6.5 × 10^15`, 0.6 % of `S0`), not a rule: a ring of the order of `Σ G` concentrates focus and `J` rises. The honest swarm's payout is unchanged by it (404,004,032 at 64 honest; 808,008,064 at 256).
- An anchored ring earns nothing up to `s = 10^12` per colluder (its attribution is still negative). From `s = 10^13` — a ring stake of 0.6–9× the honest swarm's — its attribution turns positive and it takes 93–99.99 % of the budget whenever it earns at all; the honest swarm's pulse falls pro rata, to 7 % of its lone value (k = 2, 64 honest, `R = 5.4 H`) and lower as `s` grows.
- The cartel's take relative to its stake: at most 0.056 % per epoch in the steady state and 0.197 % after ten quiet epochs (k = 2, s = 10^13, 256 honest: `R = 3 × 10^13`, `H = 5.1 × 10^13`), falling as `1/R`: 0.0003 % and 0.0009 % at `R = 6.5 × 10^15`. The per-position cap of 1 % never binds in the sweep; the budget does, and the budget is the pool's quarter, fed by the previous epoch's `focus_bps` — the cartel's own stake does not enter it.
- The gate: it does not stop the ring. With one new axon to the heaviest old particle the ring's region has `ext = 1.19 × 10^12` and the ring keeps 88–99 % of the steady budget (4.9 of 5.5 × 10^9 at `s = 10^13`, 64 honest; 5.48 of 5.52 × 10^9 at `s = 10^14`); the honest swarm's pulse returns to its lone value at `s = 10^13` and to 11–63 % of it at `s = 10^14`, because the scale is applied before a pro-rata division and only moves shares between earners. Its definition is griefable (a spoiler's new axons joining an honest endpoint make the endpoint's own weight internal) and has no on-chain test of whose attention the external weight is. The cartel bound holds with and without it; rewards.md §7 withdraws it.
- The budget itself: 5.5 × 10^9 PUSSY an epoch at the honest swarm of 64 (θ = 5 × 10^-6, 22 bps), 1.7 × 10^10 at 256 (46 bps); after ten quiet epochs 1.9 × 10^10 and 6.0 × 10^10.
