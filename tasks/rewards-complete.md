### sample-design verdict (check 2)

Head design: `π(n)` = Fisher–Yates over the hemera XOF of `D_ORDER ‖ b_E ‖ cluster(E) ‖ template`, ticket = marginals of `W = min(N, 64)` cyclic ranks from a drawn rank `r`.

- Unbiased for Shapley, including the cyclic window for N > 64: `r` is independent of `π`; the player at rank `j` of a uniform permutation has a uniform `j`; the cyclic window includes every rank with probability exactly `W/N` (no edge effect, which a non-cyclic window would have), so conditioned on inclusion `j` is still uniform and the predecessors are a uniform `j`-subset: `E[m_c | c in window] = Shapley_c`.
