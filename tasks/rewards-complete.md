### sample-design verdict (check 2)

Head design: `π(n)` = Fisher–Yates over the hemera XOF of `D_ORDER ‖ b_E ‖ cluster(E) ‖ template`, ticket = marginals of `W = min(N, 64)` cyclic ranks from a drawn rank `r`.

- Faithful to the owner's §7 on master ("each hash attempt is a Shapley sample", `π(n) = H(b_E ‖ cluster ‖ n)`, a marginal sample under `π(n)`, the swarm mean converging by Hoeffding): yes. It restores the owner's ordering; the earlier Owen draw replaced the owner's "ordering" by a coalition threshold (a rewrite of meaning, now withdrawn).
- Unbiased for Shapley, including the cyclic window for N > 64: `r` is independent of `π`; the player at rank `j` of a uniform permutation has a uniform `j`; the cyclic window includes every rank with probability exactly `W/N` (no edge effect, which a non-cyclic window would have), so conditioned on inclusion `j` is still uniform and the predecessors are a uniform `j`-subset: `E[m_c | c in window] = Shapley_c`. It is not a uniform full-permutation sample: a ticket covers `W` of `N` players and `k_c ≈ 240·64/N`. The cost is `W+1` solves, a wrap costs the same (prefix 0 is free). Owen (the earlier head) was also unbiased (`∫ ∂_c ṽ(t…t) dt`), at 2 solves per marginal (half the statistical efficiency per solve of a prefix-reusing permutation) and no per-sample efficiency identity.
- Consistent with tru#46: not yet. `epoch::ordering(beacon, cluster, nonce: u64, n)` takes an 8-byte nonce; the spec passes the 168-byte template (rewards.md:191) and says "for one reason" — the signature must change, and the pinned digest `9c3e34db…abcf` then no longer pins the chain's call. The receipt has no window function and no rank draw (`sample` is all marginals; `r` would be the XOF draw after `n−1` Fisher–Yates draws, which `ordering` does not expose). `Sample::preimage` orders marginals by player index (`m[i]` of player `i`); the spec orders them by rank from `r` (rewards.md:204): for N ≤ 64 the spec calls it "the receipt's sample" but the commitment bytes differ. Silent disagreement between spec and PR: RW-3.
- Convergence argument, withholding, DoS bound: convergence holds (RW-5 on the count); withholding is priced only for withheld winners, not for free pre-selection (RW-8); the DoS bound is false (RW-1).

### findings

RW-1 blocking — a forged sample costs the forger hashes, the verifier 65 solves. rewards.md:228 "a flood of tickets is a flood of blocks, which the target already prices"; amendments 1.11, 2.11 "producing it cost the attacker a real win". The target prices honest attempts (solves); a header whose `sample` is any 32 bytes with `id < target` costs `2^256/target` hemera hashes (128 at GENESIS_BITS, 16 at the floor; about 11 µs per 4-permutation header, chain.md §1.2: 1.4 ms to 0.2 ms) and costs every node `W+1` solves at validation step 8 (2.2 s of an M4 Max core at pussy). The amplification is of the order 10^3–10^4 at pussy and larger at the floor; step 5 relays before step 8. This is the owner's own "asymmetric-cost DoS" of the old Aggregation text, re-opened. Needed: a rule in §7 (what a node does with a header that fails step 8: bound on checks per peer and interval, no relay before the check, or a price), with the amplification stated.

RW-2 blocking — bostrom cannot validate its blocks. Verification is `W+1 = 65` cold solves (rewards.md:226, 214). Bostrom solve 6.0–6.3 s one thread plus 1.5–2.1 s build (chain-economy §7.2): about 490–530 s of one core per block, 41–45 s across twelve cores, against a 30 s interval; a node falls behind permanently. The spec states the interval claim with pussy's numbers only and for bostrom says "of the order of minutes on one thread" (:214). POW_LIMIT "the chain never stalls below one laptop" (:220) is likewise stated for pussy; at bostrom a floor block is 16 samples of 8+ minutes of a core, order ten minutes on twelve. The previous Owen head had 2 s at bostrom. Both launch chains are in the scope of §16; state and solve (a smaller `W_TICKET` as a function of the solve cost, or a condition under which bostrom runs another rule).

RW-3 blocking — spec and tru#46 disagree on three points listed above (nonce type, rank draw and window, preimage order) and the spec calls the function "the receipt's". One side must change; the spec should name the function it needs from #46 (`ordering(beacon, cluster, template: &[u8], n)`, `sample_window(order, r, W)`, a preimage in rank order) or adopt #46's as is.

RW-4 blocking — the sample space is smaller than the attempts. m(n) is a function of (π, r) only. Empty epoch (the first 240 blocks: epoch 0 is the genesis block alone): `a mod |A_E|` with fixed `u`, so 26,805 distinct values at pussy; N ≤ 7: at most N!·N. At 2^7 attempts a block and 240 blocks an epoch (3×10^4 attempts) a miner solves each distinct value once (26,805 × 0.036 s = 16 minutes on one thread), then grinds at hash speed, 10^3–10^4 times the rate of an honest solver; ASERT lifts the target against everyone else. This contradicts "every variation is a fresh ordering, no attempt reuses another's sample" (:193) and the cost basis of GENESIS_BITS. Needed: a draw with a continuum behind it for the empty and small games (the scale `u` or the path point drawn from the seed at 2^32 resolution, as the Aumann–Shapley path of epoch §4 is parametrized), or the condition under which the lottery is a lottery, stated.
